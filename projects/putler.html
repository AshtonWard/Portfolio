Putler.

An ongoing experiment in using an assistant for homelab investigation, documentation, and guided learning.

In development← All projects

The idea

Putler is my homelab assistant project built around Hermes. I’m exploring how an assistant can help explain systems, investigate problems, and preserve useful documentation.

The planned incident workflow

  1. Observe. Monitoring detects a condition worth investigating.
  2. Investigate. Bounded assistants gather evidence and explain likely causes.
  3. Review. A person reviews the findings and approves consequential changes.

Status and boundaries

The incident dispatcher and specialist investigation workflows are planned work. This page describes that design direction, not a completed autonomous incident-response system.
