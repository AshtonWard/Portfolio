The environment

My homelab brings together Proxmox VE, Linux, Docker, OPNsense, and Cisco networking. It gives me a place to explore segmentation, secure remote access, container hosting, and security monitoring.

The engineering questions

  • How should management, services, and other workloads be separated?
  • How can new hosts be configured consistently?
  • What evidence shows that a service is reachable and behaving correctly?
  • How can troubleshooting and recovery become repeatable?

What belongs in the case study

The next publication pass will add a reviewed architecture diagram, selected configurations, a troubleshooting example, and validation evidence. Operational addresses and private service details are unnecessary for explaining the design.

Current public evidence

The public Homelab repository is currently a starting point with a README. This page describes the project scope; it is not a claim that complete architecture documentation has already been published.
